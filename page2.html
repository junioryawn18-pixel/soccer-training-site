Recovery

Rest is important after training and games.

Sleeping and stretching helps the body recover faster and reduces injury risk.

  • Get 8 or more hours of sleep each night
  • Drink water throughout the day
  • Stretch for at least 10 minutes after training

Nutrition

Eating the right food helps players stay strong, recover faster, and perform consistently.

Daily Nutrition Routine

  1. Eat a balanced meal 2 hours before practice
  2. Drink water before, during, and after training
  3. Eat a protein-rich meal within 30 minutes after training

Foods to Focus On

  • Lean proteins: chicken, eggs, beans
  • Complex carbohydrates: rice, oats, whole wheat bread
  • Fruits and vegetables for vitamins and minerals
